How it works

1

Fill out the form

Complete the 3-step grant application like any normal web form. No agent required.

2

Ask your agent for help

Open the form in a WebMCP-enabled browser. Your agent can explain fields, check eligibility, and read your uploaded documents.

3

Review and accept

Anything the agent suggests shows up as a highlighted proposal. You accept or reject each change — nothing commits without your click.

Why WebMCP?

Bureaucratic forms break naive AI agents (screenshot + click simulation) and blind backend automation is dangerous — you do not want an agent submitting a grant application without you seeing the final state. WebMCP's model — tools live in the page, execute against real page state, and the human watches the same UI the agent is acting on — is a structural fit, not a bolt-on.
